Yes. And I'm spectacular.

Fortunately for you (but, more so, for me), I'm flesh, blood, and (a very special amount of) bone.

The best way to become convinced of this is to [meet me]({% link resources/for-clients/booking-guide/index.md %}) in person.

## More ways to check

If you're not ready for that, you're welcome to peruse my [thousands of posts on Bluesky]({% link links/bluesky/index.md %}), book [a video date]({% link rate/online/video-date/index.md %}), schedule [a curiosity call]({% link rate/online/curiosity-call/index.md %}), or peek at others' [reviews and testimonials]({% link _faq/about-me/do-you-have-any-reviews.md %}).

- I'm known *globally*. I [routinely travel]({% link _faq/meeting/when-will-you-be-in-my-city.md %}) to and speak at conferences, facilitate workshops, and advocate for sex worker rights, particularly against financial discrimination. Here's a [video of me speaking on a panel in Cannes, France](https://bsky.app/profile/violetrollergirl.com/post/3mixauiluxk26){:target="_blank"}.
- I'm also an author, having been [published on the Tryst Blog](https://tryst.link/blog/author/violet-rollergirl){:target="_blank"} and in a dead-tree anthology of trans and intersex sex workers called [TRANSactional Intercourse, edited by Jack Parker](https://www.jackviolet.store/product/order-transactional-intercourse){:target="_blank"}.

## Are *you* real(ly interested)?

If you still don't believe I'm real, then I'm afraid we might have to break up. "It's you, not me." 🥲

Otherwise, I welcome you to reach out with your [screening information]({% link _faq/screening/screening-process.md %}) and your [deposit]({% link _faq/booking/what-is-a-deposit.md %}) ready. These show me that *you're* real, too. 😘
